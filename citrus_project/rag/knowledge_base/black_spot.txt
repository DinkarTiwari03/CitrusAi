Disease: Citrus Black Spot

Citrus black spot is a fungal disease associated with Phyllosticta citricarpa.

Symptoms:
Symptoms may include dark spots or lesions on citrus leaves and fruit. Lesions can vary in appearance depending on disease development and environmental conditions.

Disease development:
Warm conditions, moisture and prolonged periods of leaf wetness can favor fungal disease development.

Management:
Monitor orchards regularly. Remove infected plant material where appropriate and maintain orchard sanitation. Manage fallen leaves and other plant debris according to locally recommended practices.

Prevention:
Maintain good orchard hygiene. Improve canopy airflow where appropriate. Avoid prolonged moisture on foliage when cultural practices can reduce it.

Important:
Fungicide use should be based on locally approved recommendations, disease pressure, crop stage and product labels.
