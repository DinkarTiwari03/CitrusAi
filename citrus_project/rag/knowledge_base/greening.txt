Disease: Citrus Greening / Huanglongbing (HLB)

Citrus greening, also called Huanglongbing or HLB, is a serious citrus disease associated with Candidatus Liberibacter bacteria.

Symptoms:
Symptoms can include blotchy or irregular leaf yellowing, asymmetric chlorosis, reduced tree vigor and poor fruit development. Symptoms may vary with disease stage.

Disease spread:
HLB is primarily associated with insect vectors such as the Asian citrus psyllid. Movement of infected plant material can also contribute to disease spread.

Management:
Regularly monitor citrus plants for symptoms and vector presence. Use healthy planting material. Follow local integrated HLB management recommendations.

Prevention:
Monitor and manage insect vectors according to local agricultural recommendations. Remove or manage infected trees when recommended by local authorities or agricultural specialists.

Important:
HLB management is region-specific. Chemical control and vector-management recommendations should follow local agricultural guidance and product labels.
