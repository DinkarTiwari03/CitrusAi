General Citrus Management

Regular monitoring:
Inspect citrus leaves, shoots and fruit regularly. Early detection can help identify disease development before symptoms become widespread.

Sanitation:
Remove or manage infected plant material according to locally recommended agricultural practices. Clean equipment when moving between affected and healthy plants where appropriate.

Canopy management:
Maintain appropriate canopy conditions to support airflow and reduce prolonged leaf wetness.

Planting material:
Use healthy and disease-free planting material whenever possible.

Disease management:
Disease management should combine monitoring, sanitation, cultural practices and locally approved treatments.

Chemical treatments:
Chemical recommendations must be based on locally approved products, crop conditions, disease identification, application instructions and label requirements.

Safety:
Farmers should follow product labels, local regulations and advice from qualified agricultural extension personnel before applying pesticides.

Severity interpretation:
A larger estimated affected leaf area indicates greater visible disease burden. However, image-based severity estimation is an estimate and should be combined with field inspection.
